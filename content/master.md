# 释延朋师傅 / Master Shi Yanpeng

## 中文

**释延朋师傅**
少林寺第三十四代俗家弟子

释延朋师傅是中国嵩山少林寺第三十四代俗家弟子，毕生致力于传承与弘扬正统少林武术的内涵与戒规。

他持有中国武术协会颁发的咏春拳与武术一级教练证书，并获少林武术协会、少林武术专家委员会授予六段段位，在武术修习者当中属于难得的资历。

除教学之外，延朋师傅还曾参演并指导多部功夫影片，将原汁原味的少林功夫与技法搬上银幕，向更广的大众传递中国功夫的精神。

每一堂课上，他不光讲究招式的精准，更着重引导学员修内力、立武德，追求身心合一。他耐心、通透、专注，带领学员走入鲜活的传统功夫传承。

他的初心质朴而厚重：把正宗的少林功夫与中国传统功夫传给每一位心怀诚意、意志坚定的求学者。

地址：山东省菏泽市牡丹区尧舜产业园内尧舜牡丹酒店 坤武功夫国际交流中心

## English

**Master Shi Yanpeng**
34th-generation lay disciple of the Shaolin Temple

Master Shi Yanpeng is a 34th-generation lay disciple of the Shaolin Temple on Mount Song, China. He has devoted his life to preserving and passing on the substance and the precepts of authentic Shaolin martial arts.

He holds first-class coaching certificates in Wing Chun and Wushu from the Chinese Wushu Association, and has been awarded the 6th Duan rank by the Shaolin Wushu Association and its Expert Committee — a rare level of qualification among martial artists.

Beyond teaching, he has performed in and choreographed several kung fu films, bringing genuine Shaolin technique to the screen and the spirit of Chinese kung fu to a wider audience.

In every class he demands precise technique, but his deeper focus is on cultivating inner strength, martial virtue (武德) and the unity of body and mind. Patient, clear and focused, he leads his students into a living tradition.

His aim is simple: to pass on authentic Shaolin and traditional Chinese kung fu to every student who comes with sincerity and determination.

Address: Kunwu Kung Fu International Exchange Center, Yaoshun Peony Hotel, Yaoshun Industrial Park, Mudan District, Heze, Shandong Province, China
